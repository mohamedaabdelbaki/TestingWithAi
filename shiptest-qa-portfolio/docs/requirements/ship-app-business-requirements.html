Business requirements

A shipping booking practice app where signed-in customers prepare, price, save, finalize, and manage parcel shipments.

Reviewed against main · 447c72c on 18 November 2026. Findings are based on the repository’s stories and current behavior as written.

Customer journey

1. Start a shipment

Sign in, review the account’s sender details, then enter the receiver and parcel details.

2. Choose and price

Choose an eligible service, pickup method, and additional options. Review the itemized quote.

3. Save or finalize

Save an incomplete draft or validate and finalize a complete booking.

4. Manage shipments

Review shipment history, complete drafts, repeat past shipments, or remove a record.

Primary actor: The signed-in customer owns and manages shipments. Sender and receiver are shipment contacts. The repository describes a QA learning app. The shipment record covers booking through finalization. Payment, carrier handoff, live parcel tracking, delivery events, returns, and refunds have no defined workflow in the reviewed scope.

Requirements by business area

BR-01 Account and access

An account is registered with unique email (case-insensitive), password of at least 6 characters, full name, phone, country, city, street and postal code. Registration signs the customer in. Returning customers sign in with their email and password and can sign out. Account sessions are configured for 30 days. Shipment records and assistant shipment lookups are scoped to the signed-in owner. Account name and email are visible while signed in. Profile editing, password recovery and account administration have no defined customer workflow.

Story group 01 · Story group 10

BR-02 Sender and receiver

A new shipment takes sender contact and address details from the customer account and presents a read-only summary. The customer supplies the receiver’s full name, phone, country, city and postal code. Street is mandatory for either party whose country is in the Gulf group. For non-Gulf shipment addresses it is optional. The form expects names and cities of at least 2 characters, phone numbers with 10–15 digits, postal codes of 3–10 characters and a Gulf street of at least 5 characters. The stories describe letters, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes for names. See open decisions for inconsistent validation.

BR-03 Route eligibility and shipment type

Same country means Domestic, including same-country Gulf routes. Two different Gulf countries mean IntraGulf. All other country pairs are International. Gulf origins cannot ship to Iraq. The reverse direction is permitted subject to the usual International rules. An origin outside the Gulf shipping into the Gulf requires an item description. Story group 03 · Story group 09

BR-04 Parcel details and limits

Weight in kilograms and length, width and height in centimeters are required. The form rules specify at least 0.1 kg and 1–200 cm per dimension. The maximum parcel weights are 50 kg Domestic, 30 kg IntraGulf and 25 kg International. An item description is shown and required for non-Gulf to Gulf and the form specifies 5–200 characters. A service may have a lower limit than the route. Exact maxima are inclusive.

Story group 04 · Story group 09

BR-05 Service selection

Offer services for the calculated shipment type and package weight. Show each option’s name, delivery estimate and price information. A customer selects one eligible service. If a change makes the selected service unavailable, clear the selection. If no services qualify, present a clear message. The service matrix below includes delivery estimates in business days.

Story group 05 · Story group 09

BR-06 Pickup and additional options

Home pickup and postal-office drop-off are available by default, with home pickup preselected. Above 17 kg, drop-off is required except for shipments originating in Iraq. At exactly 17 kg, home pickup is allowed. All shipments to Jordan or Egypt require signature, including domestic shipments, and the signature option is checked and locked. Other destinations can opt into signature. Liquid handling, insurance and professional packaging are optional and add fixed charges when selected.

Story group 06 · Story group 09

BR-07 Quote and final price

The shipment quote updates with the service, weight, pickup and selected options. The base line combines the service charge and origin-based pickup fee. Option fees are added separately. Totals display to two decimal places. Finalization recalculates the price from shipment details and current pricing. Where a draft has an old or zero quote, the finalized price reflects the current rules.

Story group 07

BR-08 Progressive booking form

Receiver details follow the sender summary. Valid receiver details unlock package entry. Completed package details unlock service selection. In the current form the options area also unlocks at that point, and the quote needs a service. Required fields and validation errors guide the user, with further checks on finalization. Incomplete records can be saved as drafts.

Story group 11

BR-09 Draft and finalization lifecycle

A draft may contain partial details. Edit drafts to resume work, and finalize a draft from its list or detail actions once it passes the booking and price checks. A new completed shipment can be finalized directly. Finalized records are read-only. A shipment gets a tracking reference at creation (TR followed by nine digits), including when first saved as a draft. Finalizing an existing draft keeps its reference. The reference identifies the record.

Story group 08

BR-10 History, repeat and removal

My Shipments shows the owner’s records newest first, with tracking reference, route, weight, status, price and actions. The detail view shows contacts, parcel, service, options and rate. Filters cover status and shipment type. Repeat pre-fills a new booking using a finalized shipment, leaving the original record in place. Confirm before removing a shipment. Removing a draft is presented as deletion and removing a finalized shipment is presented as cancellation. Both remove the record.

Story group 08

BR-11 Shipping and policy assistants

In the create, edit or repeat form, the optional assistant can look up the signed-in owner’s shipments, check rules, calculate quotes, explain published policy, review entered values and point out missing fields. It can propose receiver, parcel, pickup and option changes for the customer to review and apply. Sender details stay protected. A separate documentation assistant offers example questions and source-based answers. The assistants should acknowledge missing information and never invent shipments, prices, or policies. Core shipment workflows remain usable if the assistant is unavailable.
